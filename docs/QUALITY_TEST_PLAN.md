# Quality Test Plan

## Photo scenes
- daylight landscape
- indoor mixed lighting
- skin
- hair
- text
- brick/fabric
- foliage
- backlit subject
- neon/night street
- 1–5 lux
- moving person
- pets
- cars
- water
- tripod dark scene

## Metrics
- SNR
- PSNR/SSIM where reference exists
- MTF50/detail
- color ΔE
- ghosting
- highlight clipping
- shadow recovery
- temporal artifacts
- RAW linearity

## Video
- resolution/FPS verification with decoded timestamps
- long-duration dropped frames
- encoder bitrate
- HDR metadata
- audio sync
- stabilization
- rolling shutter
- lens switching
- thermal throttling
